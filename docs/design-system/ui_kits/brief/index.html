why splitting things up matters05 / 09
why splitting things up matters

One monolith → four specialists.

  • Traditional C2: one system did everything — single point of failure.
  • Shared bus splits the jobs apart — each piece small, replaceable, doing one thing well.
  • A new entrant does not rebuild the world. Shows up, does its one thing, plugs in.
  • The thing that gathers data isn't the thing that decides who sees it.

how it fits into goat-net06 / 09
how it fits into goat-net

Bus inside the envelope. Identity at the door.

  • Bus runs inside the private, encrypted network. Network handles secrecy; Zenoh handles the data.
  • Every connecting system carries a unique digital identity — a passport.
  • Identity says which names it can publish under and receive on.
  • A vendor publishes only under their own area. Operators control who reads what.
